Garytak Support

Last updated October 9, 2026.

Garytak is an on-device calendar short-clip diary. Clips, captions, recap movies, gift packs, and balance stay in this install. There is no account and no cloud library.

In-app help

Open Settings for Appearance, Terms of Use, Privacy Policy, and Clear All Data. Legal pages open inside the app. Use Capture to record or import, Recap to preview and compile, and Days to browse sample days.

Purchases

Gift packs and treasure chests are Consumable In-App Purchases processed by Apple. This version does not offer Restore Purchases. Preview does not spend balance. Compile spends balance only after you confirm and a movie is written.

Permissions

After you agree to Terms and Privacy, Garytak shows the system notification dialog on the first calendar visit. Camera, microphone, speech recognition, and Photos are requested only when you tap Record, Speak Caption, Import, or Save Video.

Contact

For App Store review questions, use the review notes bundled with this build, or this Support page.
