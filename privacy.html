Garytak Privacy Policy

Last updated October 9, 2026.

Garytak keeps your diary on the device where you installed it. The app does not create an account and does not upload clips to a Garytak server.

Data on this device

  • Daily clips, captions, caption style, drafts, recap movies, the on-device balance, and verified purchase identifiers so the same transaction is not applied twice.
  • Light or dark appearance, and whether the first-run calendar dialogs and the New User Gift Pack have already been answered.
  • The Days tab shows five built-in October clips. They are not collected from you.

What Garytak does not collect

Garytak does not collect an account, email address, contacts, location, or analytics. It does not share diary files with a Garytak server or an advertising network.

Camera, microphone, and photos

The camera is requested only when you tap Record, to capture that day’s 3, 5, or 12 second clip. The microphone is requested only when you tap Speak Caption, so spoken words can fill that caption. Speech recognition, when used for that caption, stays with that action. Photo library access is requested only when you import a clip you pick or tap Save Video. If you decline, you can type a caption, leave the movie in the app, or skip the blocked action. The app does not scan your library in the background and does not upload camera, microphone, or photo data.

Tracking and notifications

The first time the calendar is shown, Garytak displays the system App Tracking Transparency dialog, then the system notification dialog. Tracking Allow or Ask App Not to Track only records that choice. The notification Allow and Don’t Allow only dismiss that dialog. This version does not send notifications. Neither choice changes Days, your calendar, or a recap.

Purchases

Gift packs and treasure chests are consumable in-app purchases sold by Apple. Apple processes the payment. Garytak stores the verified transaction identifier and the balance on this device. The first visit can claim a New User Gift Pack of 10, stored only on this device. The balance cannot be restored after the app is deleted or its data is cleared.

Other parties

Apple is the seller of in-app purchases. Garytak does not send your clips, captions, or balance to any other company.

Clearing data

Clear All Data removes clips, movies, drafts, preferences, purchase records stored in the app, and the balance from this install. There is no account to delete.

Contact

Privacy questions stay in the app’s Support note. No external support address was provided with the product materials.
